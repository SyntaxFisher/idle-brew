Idle Brew is a small, free menu bar app for macOS 13 and later.

- Toggle a gentle one-pixel mouse movement every 30 seconds from the coffee-cup icon.
- Optionally start Idle Brew when you sign in.
- Receive automatic updates, or check for updates from the menu.
- Includes Apple silicon and Intel support in one signed and notarized app.

Open the DMG, drag Idle Brew to Applications, and launch it. On first launch, allow Idle Brew in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.
